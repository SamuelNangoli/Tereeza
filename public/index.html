A little clarity

Before we
get started.

How much will it cost?

The cost depends on your device and the issue. Share the details on WhatsApp so we can discuss the work and agree on a price before getting started.

Can you help remotely?

Some software and connectivity problems can be discussed or guided remotely. Hardware issues may need an in-person check. We’ll agree on a suitable arrangement in the chat.

What should I include in my message?

Tell us your device model, the issue, when it started and any error message. You can add a photo or screenshot in WhatsApp if it helps explain what you’re seeing.

What if I don’t know what’s wrong?

That’s a good place to start. Choose “Not sure” in the form and describe what you’ve noticed. You don’t need to know the technical name.
